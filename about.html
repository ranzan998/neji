This website was created to make the often time-consuming task of finding academic and research positions easier, across all career stages. Inspired by resources like the MetJobs mailing list and developed in collaboration with the Early Career Ocean Professional (ECOP) community, the platform was built by Ranjan Kumar Sahu, Trishneeta Bhattacharya, and Pooja Tiwari. Our goal is to provide an accessible, well-organized platform for sharing relevant opportunities. You are welcome to submit or share job postings, conferences, events, symposiums, or workshops to help maximize their reach. We also welcome your feedback, please feel free to contact us at ranzan(dot)ocean(at)gmail.com.

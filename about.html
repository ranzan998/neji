This website was created to simplify the often tedious process of searching for academic and research positions across all tiers. Inspired in part by resources like the MetJobs mailing list, it aims to provide a more accessible and organized platform for finding relevant opportunities. You're free to submit or share info about job postings, conference, events, symposiums or workshops for maximum reach. Your feedback is welcome—feel free to reach out at ranzan(dot)ocean(at)gmail.com.
